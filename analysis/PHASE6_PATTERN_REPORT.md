# UPSC CSE 2028 PYQ Research — Phase 6 Pattern Analysis

## Scope
This analysis uses the verified AIR 1–5 marks dataset for 2019–2025, the current 2016–2026 core paper inventory, and the 2026 Prelims question-slot staging register. OCR-staged question slots are not treated as validated question text.

## Statistical results
- AIR 1–5 observations: 35.
- AIR 1 final-score linear slope: -1.107 marks/year; R² = 0.010. Descriptive historical fit only.
- Rank vs final score Pearson correlation: -0.644; Spearman: -0.640.
- Optional-subject HHI across the 35 AIR 1–5 observations: 0.1184.
- Most frequent optional share: 0.200; top-three optional share: 0.514.
- 2026 question slots staged: 180; validated/promoted question records: 0.

## Gate
Question-level recurrence, syllabus mapping, semantic similarity, difficulty modeling and interdisciplinary tagging remain blocked until OCR segmentation and official answer-key validation are complete.
